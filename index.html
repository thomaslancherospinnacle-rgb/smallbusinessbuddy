Questions

FREQUENTLY
ASKED

How does MapLeads actually find businesses without websites?
MapLeads uses a Tampermonkey browser extension that reads Google Maps results in real time. It checks whether each business has a website linked in their Maps listing, then cross-checks with a live Google search to catch any sites they didn't add to Maps.
Do I need any coding skills or API keys?
None. Install Tampermonkey (free browser extension), paste the MapLeads script, and you're done. No API keys, no developer account, no backend to manage. It works directly inside your browser on Google Maps.
What counts as a "lead" toward my monthly limit?
A lead is any business that passes both the chain filter and the Google verification check — meaning it's confirmed to have no website. Businesses with websites are not counted against your limit.
Can I use this for any business category or city?
Yes. You search Google Maps for whatever you want — restaurants in Dallas, plumbers in Chicago, nail salons in Miami — MapLeads works on any category in any location. Results depend on what Google Maps has indexed for that area.
Is this against Google's Terms of Service?
MapLeads reads publicly visible information from Google Maps — the same data anyone can see by scrolling through search results. It does not scrape in a way that bypasses authentication, violates rate limits, or accesses private data. Use it responsibly for legitimate sales prospecting.
Can multiple team members share one plan?
Yes. The Unlimited plan is designed for teams. Anyone you share your Google Sheet and Tampermonkey script with can scan and push leads to the same pipeline. The Unlimited plan has no seat limits.
